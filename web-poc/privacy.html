JellyShell

Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

The short version

  • JellyShell talks only to servers you choose: your own Jellyfin server and, if you use them, your own Seerr and home server.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.
  • Diagnostic logs go to your own server, never to us.
  • Your sign-in details stay on your console.

Who we are

JellyShell is a client for your own Jellyfin media server, with a home dashboard, for Xbox. It is open-source software; "we" below means the publisher of the build you installed. JellyShell is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Jellyfin project.

What JellyShell connects to

JellyShell does not run any servers that collect your data. It connects only to:

  • Your Jellyfin server, to sign you in, browse your library, show artwork and play your media.
  • Your Seerr server (optional), if your server owner has set up media requests.
  • Your home server (optional), which hosts the app's interface, the home dashboard and the diagnostic log described below.

These servers are run by you or by whoever invited you to use them. What they store is governed by their owner, not by us. Some request screens show poster artwork that your Seerr server links from The Movie Database's image service (image.tmdb.org); loading those images sends your network address to that service, as with any web image.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, sell or share personal information. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not build a profile of you.

Information stored on your console

To keep you signed in, JellyShell stores your server address, your Jellyfin user name and user ID and the access token your Jellyfin server issues, along with your app preferences (for example audio, subtitle and display settings). This information stays in the app's local storage on your console. Signing out removes the access token; uninstalling the app removes all of it. Your password is sent only to your own Jellyfin server and is not stored by the app.

Diagnostic logs

To help fix playback problems, JellyShell sends short technical log lines (for example app version, device type, playback events and error messages) to the home server the app's interface is loaded from. These logs stay on that server. They are never sent to us or to any third party, and access keys are removed from them before they are written.

Microsoft

JellyShell is distributed through the Microsoft Store and runs on Xbox. Microsoft may collect information about the console, the Store and app installs under its own privacy statement. We do not receive any of it beyond the aggregate reports Partner Center gives every publisher.

Children

JellyShell is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. What content is available depends entirely on the media library and parental controls of your own Jellyfin server.
